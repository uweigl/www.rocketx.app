RocketX vs Pepperi

Pepperi deserves respect: 1,000+ customers, true native iOS and Android apps, offline order-taking for field reps. It is the closest shape to RocketX on the market — which is exactly why the differences are worth stating precisely.

What Pepperi genuinely does well

A mature field-sales suite: rep order-taking on tablets, trade promotions, SAP Business One integration, two decades of wholesale domain knowledge across food, beauty and consumer goods. If your model is rep-led van sales with deep trade-promotion mechanics, Pepperi has features RocketX does not try to match.

The documented edges

  • Offline is Pepperi's most-praised feature — and sync reliability is its most-repeated complaint. Recent reviews describe crashes, freezes and resync failures during store visits, and update-related downtime.Source: Capterra — Pepperi reviews
  • The public pricing page is gone — both URL variants return 404 since the 2024 private-equity acquisition. Third-party sources describe per-user licences plus modules plus implementation, with real mid-market costs reported at $20,000–50,000+/year.Source: WizCommerce — Pepperi pricing analysis
  • Support is time-zone bound, reviewers report, with slower Android parity and a back office one French reviewer calls “vieillissant et catastrophiquement lent”.Source: Capterra / G2 — Pepperi reviews

Take these three questions to their demo

  1. What happens to a half-taken order when the resync fails mid-visit?
  2. What is the all-in bill at our user count — licences, modules, implementation?
  3. Who answers when it breaks at 7am our time?

Where RocketX pulls ahead

  • A true native app experience: iOS and Android apps built for B2B ordering — not a website wrapped in a shell.
  • One cart on every surface: a full web app in any browser on any computer or device, plus the native apps — the same server-side cart everywhere, cached on the device and synced back the moment the connection returns.
  • Rich media where the order happens: print brochures as PDFs and product video live beside the catalogue — and still open with no signal.
  • Barcode scanning into the cart: point at the shelf or the box and the line is in the order.
  • Quality checks before the order leaves: the cart flags recent duplicates and attributes every change to a name and a timestamp.
  • Implemented by the people who built it: RocketX sets the platform up for you directly — no agency in between, no agency fees, no hand-offs where quality slips, and support from the team that knows the code.
  • Orders come together faster: search answers under a second at your real catalogue size, and the whole team builds one cart at once — the pilot measures the difference on your own numbers.

Where they are the right choice

Choose Pepperi if your business is rep-driven van sales with heavy trade-promotion needs and you have the implementation partner its setup expects. If the centre of your problem is buyers and reps building orders together — reliably, offline, at a predictable price — that is the problem RocketX was built around.

The way to settle it is not a comparison page — ours included. It is a 30–45 day pilot against your own catalogue, with measures agreed up front. If the pilot misses them, you walk away owing nothing.
