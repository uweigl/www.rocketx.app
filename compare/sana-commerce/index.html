RocketX vs Sana Commerce

Sana Commerce made a clear architectural bet: the webstore reads and writes your ERP directly, in real time. Where that bet pays off it pays well. The honest comparison is about what the same bet costs.

What Sana genuinely does well

If you run SAP or Microsoft Dynamics and want the storefront to be a pure window onto the ERP — prices, stock, customer terms, live — Sana's integration depth is its deserved reputation, and its case-study library shows real results at real manufacturers.

The documented edges, from Sana's own pages and reviewers

  • SAP and Microsoft Dynamics only. Sana's architecture requires one of two ERP families; every wholesaler on anything else is out of scope by design.Source: Sana Commerce — homepage
  • The coupling cuts both ways: reviewers report that customisation must route through the ERP, storefront performance depends on ERP web services, custom features take months, and upgrades break customer-specific work.Source: PeerSpot — Sana Commerce pros and cons
  • “They only check cases twice a week” — the same support-cadence complaint appears verbatim on two independent review platforms, alongside reports that support is effectively English-only for non-English markets.Source: G2 / TrustRadius — Sana Commerce reviews
  • No native buyer app in the current product. Sana Commerce Cloud is a web storefront; the offline mobile app exists only in legacy 9.0 documentation as a sales-agent tool.Source: Sana Commerce 9.0 documentation — Sana Mobile
  • Pricing is quote-only across all three tiers — no number appears anywhere on the pricing page.Source: Sana Commerce — pricing page

Where they are the right choice

Choose Sana if you are committed to SAP or Dynamics, want the storefront to be the ERP's mirror, and your buyers order at desks on the web. If your buyers order from aisles and cars, your team builds orders together, or your ERP is not on Sana's list — that is the gap RocketX fills, without asking you to replace anything.

The way to settle it is not a comparison page — ours included. It is a 30–45 day pilot against your own catalogue, with measures agreed up front. If the pilot misses them, you walk away owing nothing.
